Grandview Clubs

The Grandview Bethesda has a thriving network of clubs that continues to grow. See the list below for details.

Don't see a club for your interests? Start one yourself! Contact Resident Services at 301-272-9654 to get started. Once your new club is formed, contact the Grandview Exchange Administrator to set up a subgroup on Grandview Exchange. Being on Grandview Exchange makes it easy to send messages to club members, coordinate activities with a group calendar, and more!

Computer/IT Club

The Computer/IT Club, created in July 2023, was one of the first Grandview clubs. As of February 26. 2026, we have 34 members. The Club is a forum for members to become more knowledgeable about the use of their electronic devices - such as computers, laptops, tablets (like iPads), and smartphones. The Club's members range from novices on tech issues to people who have been in the tech business or who have been speakers on tech and AI topics at local senior educational centers, such as the Osher Life-Long Learning Institutes at American University and Johns Hopkins.
  • Club Leader: Sheldon Hochberg
  • Computer/IT Club Subgroup on Grandview Exchange

Fiction Book Clubs

The fiction book clubs are made up of participants, led by a facilitator, who value open discussion, respect for differing opinions, and are open to reading a variety of books from short story anthologies, to best sellers, to classics, to whatever piques our interest in the world of fiction. We will collectively select books to read; one for each month, several months in advance.

There are multiple fiction book clubs at The Grandview. Club membership is so popular, the club leaders decided to form multiple clubs with a maximum of 15 members. To join a club, please join the Fiction Book Club Lobby.

The Lobby allows residents to express interest in joining the next available club. Resident Services is eager to hear from you as to whether YOU would like to serve as a future Book Club Coordinator. If you are interested, please call them at 301-272-9654.

Once a new club opens for business, the club leader selects participants from The LOBBY.

  • Fiction Book Club Lobby Subgroup on Grandview Exchange

The Grandview Jewish Congregation

The Grandview Jewish Congregation fosters a welcoming, spiritually vibrant, and inclusive Jewish community for the residents of The Grandview Senior Living in Bethesda, Maryland. Rooted in Jewish tradition and guided by the values of kehillah (community), chesed (kindness), and tikkun olam (repairing the world), we provide meaningful worship, lifelong learning, pastoral support, and opportunities for connection. We welcome residents of all backgrounds, levels of observance, and abilities. Our mission is to enrich the lives of residents through accessible, uplifting, and compassionate religious and communal experiences, including Shabbat and holiday services. Announcements will be sent via email and posted under “The Grandview Jewish Congregation” in My Erickson Clubs and Groups. High Holiday services this year will be held on the First Day of Rosh Hashana (September 12th), Kol Nidre, and Yom Kippur (September 20th & 21st) in the Life Enrichment Center. Dues to the Congregation are still being determined. Look for information in a future announcement.

  • Contact: Helen Sonenshine

Mah Jongg Club

Mah Jongg is more than just a game—it’s a wonderful way to keep the mind active and give those brain cells a little rejuvenation each week. Just as importantly, it’s an opportunity to build camaraderie, enjoy good conversation, and strengthen friendships with neighbors around the table. The 2026 Mah Jongg cards will be released at the end of March and can be ordered online through the National Mah Jongg League (www.nationalmahjonggleague.org). No charge for shipping! Be sure to have your new card ready as we begin another season of play. If you have a Mah Jongg set, please feel free to bring it, too. Come join the fun and festivities at The Grandview. Pull up a chair, shuffle the tiles, and let the games begin!

  • Club Leader: Alice Brauer
  • Mah Jongg Club Subgroup on Grandview Exchange

Modern American Canasta

Good friends, good times, good canasta! We invite residents of all ages to join in on the fun. Play as often as you like, your involvement is your choice. The group meets every Monday, from 1:00 pm to 3:00 pm at the Card Room in Potomac Heights. We are playing by the rules endorsed by the Canasta League of America (CLA). The Canasta Junction website (https://canastajunction.com) as well as the mobile Canasta Junction application, available on the Apple App Store and Google Play, are excellent places to practice based on the CLA rules. Canasta Junction is free for solitaire use. Whether you are a very experienced player or just learning the game, we welcome everyone to come and play. We help new players learn the game and enjoy the experience. We foster connection, friendship, camaraderie and most of all fun. Join us in building a community where friendships flourish and shared interests bring us closer together. Together, lets make Modern American Canasta a place where everyone feels welcome and at home. Helpful information about our Canasta rules can be found by clicking on the Files menu on the left.

  • Club Leaders: Judy Zabell, Jim Silva
  • Modern American Canasta Subgroup on Grandview Exchange
