Create Your Grandview Exchange Profile

Maximize your presence within Grandview Exchange by filling out your member profile. Once customized, your user profile will be visible across all Grandview Exchange groups and subgroups that you actively join, helping neighbors get to know you.

Steps to Create or Update Your Profile

Follow the steps below to create or update your Grandview Exchange profile..

  1. Access Account Settings: Go to the main Grandview Exchange Home Group interface and click on your personal name link located in the far upper right-hand corner of the webpage.
  2. Open the Settings Menu: Click on the Settings option on the dropdown menu.
  3. Navigate to Identify: Click on the Identity link on the left side of the page.
  4. Enter Profile Details: Update the fields on the screen. (You don't need to fill out every field).
  5. Set Your Profile Privacy: Locate the Profile Privacy setting and change it to Other members of your groups.

    IMPORTANT - Do not set your profile privacy to Public. This would allow anyone to see your profile even if they are not a member of Grandview Exchange
