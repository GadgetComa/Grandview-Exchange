Getting Started with Your Browser

Using a browser gives you the full Grandview Exchange website on a larger screen. This is the easiest way to read messages and explore the site.

Is the Browser a Good Choice for You?

Choose the browser if you want to see more on the screen at one time and prefer the familiarity of a web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).

  • A familiar way to access web sites on a computer or tablet
  • Easier to see more information at once
  • Requires using links or using bookmarks to get to the site

Using Your Browser to Access Grandview Exchange

You can use your favorite browser on any device.

Access the Grandview Exchange website:

  • Click this link to go to Grandview Exchange in your browser, or
  • Open your browser and enter grandviewexchange.groups.io.

If you want to make it easy to get back to the site, you can create a bookmark/favorite or add Grandview Exchange to your desktop or home screen.

  • If you want to create a bookmark or favorite, follow these instructions.
  • If you want to add Grandview Exchange to your desktop or home screen, follow these instructions.

Once Grandview Exchange opens in your browser, you may need to log in with your Grandview Exchange account.

  • If you don't have an account yet, click this link to register.
  • If you need help logging in, click this link for assistance.

Once you log in, you will see the list of the groups you have joined and buttons on the bottom (on phones and other small screens) or a menu on the left (for tablets like an iPad). The buttons and menus let you quickly access other groups, messages, the calendar (if your group uses it), and more.

Tap on the group you want to view and you will see the messages posted in that group.
