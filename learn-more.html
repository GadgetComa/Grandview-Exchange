What is Grandview Exchange?

Grandview Exchange is the online community created by and for residents, reservists, and Priority List members of The Grandview Bethesda. It’s a place where neighbors can share information, ask questions, stay informed, and connect with one another — all in one easy-to-use space. Think of it as a digital neighborhood.

Just like chatting in the lobby or talking with friends at dinner, Grandview Exchange helps you:

  • See what’s happening around the community
  • Ask for recommendations or advice
  • Share announcements or items for sale
  • Join conversations about daily life at The Grandview and other topics you're interested in
  • Stay connected with friends and neighbors

How Grandview Exchange is Organized

Grandview Exchange is organized into groups, each serving a different purpose.

The Home Group

The Grandview Exchange Home Group is the main gathering place for the entire community. This is where most conversations happen — questions, announcements, helpful tips, items for sale, and general discussions among residents. If you’re ever unsure where to post something, the Home Group is almost always the right place.

Subgroups for Clubs

The Grandview has a growing number of resident‑led clubs — photography, book discussions, games, current events, and more. Each club has its own subgroup inside Grandview Exchange. These subgroups give club members a dedicated space to:

  • Share updates
  • Organize activities
  • Post photos or files
  • Coordinate meetings
  • Talk about club‑specific topics

Subgroups make it easy for club members to stay connected without cluttering the main Home Group.
