Dining Services Quick Guide

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW AT A GLANCE!

How & where can I eat?

We serve Continental Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner. West of Wisconsin Café is our quick service café that offers walk-up carryout and dine-in. Crescent Overlook is our full-service restaurant that offers pre-order carryout or delivery and dine-in. The View at 140 is our elevated but casual full-service restaurant offering dine-in lunch and dinner.

How can I view a menu?

Menus are available via My Erickson under the "Dining Information" icon. Menus will be posted one week at a time.

Is nutrition information available?

My Nutrition is an Erickson Senior Living tool to help residents find all nutritional information for our menus. Access My Nutrition using the link on My Erickson under the "Dining Information" icon.

Carryout & Delivery

To place a carryout or delivery order from Crescent Overlook, please call (301) 272-9670 by 3:00PM Monday – Saturday and by 10:00AM on Sundays. Pick up from the carryout room located in the hallway between Crescent Overlook and the West of Wisconsin Café.

Delivery meals can be ordered the same way. Please note a delivery fee of $7.00 will apply.

Reservations

What restaurants can I make a reservation for?

Dinner reservations are available at the Crescent Overlook restaurant. Seating at the Crescent Overlook bar and outdoor patio will be available on a first come, first serve basis.

Lunch reservations are available at The View at 140 restaurant.

Are reservations required?

Reservations are not required at the West of Wisconsin Cafe or the Crescent Overlook. Walk up seating is available in both restaurants.

Reservations are required at The View at 140.

How do I make a reservation

Reservations can be made using the link on My Erickson under the "Dining Information" icon. You can also call (301) 272-9670 from 10:00AM – 1:00PM daily.

If forwarded to voicemail when calling, please leave your first and last name, phone number, desired reservation time and party size. Reservations will be confirmed with a return phone call.

How many days will I be able to make reservations for?

You are able to make daily reservations online and over the phone up to six days in advance, any day of the week.

Example: If you call or go online on a Tuesday, you will be able to make a reservation for that Tuesday through the following Monday.

What is the "Get on the Wait List" option and how do I use it?

The online waitlist can be used for anyone who does not have a reservation and would like to a do a walk-in. By joining the wait list via My Erickson under the "Dining Information" icon, you can get in line before having to check in at the restaurant host stand and alleviate your wait time. You are not required to use the Wait List feature for walk-in seating.
