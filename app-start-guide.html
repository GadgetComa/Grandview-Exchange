Getting Started with the Groups.io App

The Groups.io app is the easiest way to use Grandview Exchange on a smartphone or tablet. It keeps everything in one place and makes it simple to read messages, post, and stay connected.

Getting Started with the App

The Groups.io app keeps everything in one place and makes it simple to read messages, post, and stay connected on the go.

Is the App a Good Choice for You?

Choose the app if you want:

  • Quick access from your home screen
  • A simple, mobile‑friendly layout
  • Notifications when new messages arrive
  • An easy way to read and post on the go

Installing and Getting to Know the App

The Groups.io app is available for download on Apple and Android devices. You can install it using the links below:

  • Apple App Store (for iPhone and iPad)
  • Google Play Store (for Android devices)

Once installed, open the app and sign in with your Grandview Exchange account.

  • If you don't have an account yet, click this link to register.
  • If you need help logging in, click this link for assistance.

Once you log in, you will see the list of the groups you have joined and buttons on the bottom (on phones and other small screens) or a menu on the left (for tablets like an iPad). The buttons and menus let you quickly access other groups, messages, the calendar (if your group uses it), and more.

Tap on the group you want to view and you will see the messages posted in that group.
