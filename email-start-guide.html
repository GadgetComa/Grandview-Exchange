Is Email a Good Choice for You?

Choose email if you want read, post, and reply to messages using email.

  • Use Grandview Exchange with familiarity of your favorite email application
  • Grandview Exchange messages come directly to your inbox
  • Post and reply just like any other email

Select Your Email Preference

Grandview Exchange can send messages directly to your email inbox. You can choose how many emails you receive and what each email contains.

To change your email preference:

  • Click this link to go to Grandview Exchange in your browser, or
  • Open your browser and enter grandviewexchange.groups.io.

Once Grandview Exchange opens in your browser, you may need to log in with your Grandview Exchange account.

  • If you don't have an account yet, click this link to register.
  • If you need help logging in, click this link for assistance.

Once you log in, on the left side of the page, click Subscription. Email settings are right at the top of the page. Choose the option that works best for you:

Individual Messages - You receive every message as soon as it is posted.

  • Best if you want real-time updates.
  • Each email contains the full text of one message.

Full Featured Digest - You receive one email per day containing the complete text of the most recent 12 messages.

  • Best if you want fewer emails but still want to read everything.
  • Includes the full text of the messages.

Daily Summary - You receive one email per day listing only the subject lines of all messages posted that day.

  • Best if you want a quick overview.
  • You click a link to read any message you're interested in.

Special Notices Only - You receive only important announcements from the group owner.

  • Best if you want minimal email.

Once you've made your selection, scroll all the way to the bottom of the page and click Save. Your new email settings will take effect immediately. You will now begin receiving messages according to your chosen settings and are ready to read, reply, and post using email

Reading Individual Message Emails

Individual message emails include the entire message and any attachments posted to Grandview Exchange and several links to access and manage the message. Here’s an explanation of what you'll see:

What the email looks like

  • The subject line begins with the group name in square brackets, followed by the subject line of the message. For example, [Bocce Club] Bocce cancelled due to rain.
  • The body of the email contains the full text of the message.
  • Any photos or attachments included in the original post will appear in the email

Links included in the email

You will see several links at the bottom of the email that let you access or manage the message

  • View/Reply Online - Opens the message on the website and lets you reply there.
  • Reply to Group - Sends an email reply to the entire group.
  • Reply to Sender - Sends a private email reply only to the person who posted the message.
  • Mute This Topic — Stops future emails for this specific conversation.
  • New Topic — Opens a new blank message form on the website.
  • Your Subscription — Opens your email delivery settings for this group.
  • Contact Group Owner — Sends a private message to the group owner.
  • Unsubscribe — Lets you leave the group or stop receiving emails.

Reading Full Digest Emails

With the full digest emails, you receive one email per day containing the full text of up to 12 recent messages. Here’s an explanation of what you'll see:

What the full digest looks like

  • The top of the email lists the subjects of the messages included.
  • Below the list, the digest contains the full text of each message.
  • Photos and attachments appear with the messages they belong to.

Links included in the full digest

You will see several links at the bottom of the email that let you access or manage the message

  • View/Reply Online - Opens the message on the website and lets you reply there.
  • Reply to Group - Sends an email reply to the entire group.
  • Reply to Sender - Sends a private email reply only to the person who posted the message.
  • Mute This Topic — Stops future emails for this specific conversation.
  • New Topic — Opens a new blank message form on the website.
  • Your Subscription — Opens your email delivery settings for this group.
  • Contact Group Owner — Sends a private message to the group owner.
  • Unsubscribe — Lets you leave the group or stop receiving emails.

How to read and reply to messages in the full digest

  • Scroll through the digest to read each message.
  • To reply to a specific message, use the Reply to Group, Reply to Sender, or View/Reply Online link in that message’s section.

Reading Daily Summary Emails

With the daily summary emails, You receive one email per day containing only the subject lines of all messages posted that day. Here’s an explanation of what you'll see:

What the daily summary looks like

  • A simple list of subjects and senders.
  • No message text is included.

Links included in the daily summary

You will see several links included with each subject line in the daily summary:

  • View/Reply Online - Opens the message on the website and lets you reply there.
  • Reply to Group - Sends an email reply to the entire group.
  • Reply to Sender - Sends a private email reply only to the person who posted the message.
  • Mute This Topic — Stops future emails for this specific conversation.
  • New Topic — Opens a new blank message form on the website.
  • Your Subscription — Opens your email delivery settings for this group.
  • Contact Group Owner — Sends a private message to the group owner.
  • Unsubscribe — Lets you leave the group or stop receiving emails.

How to read and reply to messages in the daily summary

  • Click View/Reply Online next to any subject you want to read.
  • This opens the full message on the Grandview Exchange website.
