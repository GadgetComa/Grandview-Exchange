Getting Started with Email

Grandview Exchange lets you use email to read, post, and reply to messages just as easily as you do for any other email messages.

Getting Started with Email

You can receive Grandview Exchange messages directly in your email inbox and reply just like any other email.

Is Email a Good Choice for You?

Choose email if you want read, post, and reply to messages using email.

  • Use Grandview Exchange with familiarity of your favorite email application
  • Grandview Exchange messages come directly to your inbox
  • Post and reply just like any other email

Choose How to Receive Grandview Exchange Messages by Email

Grandview Exchange can send messages directly to your email inbox. You can choose how many emails you receive and what each email contains. Your choices are:

Individual Messages

You receive every message as soon as it is posted.

  • Best if you want real-time updates.
  • Each email containst the full text of one message.

Full Featured Digest

You receive one email per day containing the complete text of the most recent 12 messages.

  • Best if you want fewer emails but still want to read everything.
  • Includes the full text of the messages.

Daily Summary

You receive one email per day listing only the subject lines of all messages posted that day.

  • Best if you want a quick overview.
  • You click a link to read any message you're interested in.

Special Notices Only

You receive only important annoucements from the group owner.

  • Best if you want minimal email.

Set Your Email Delivery Settings

To change your email delivery settings:

  • Click this link to go to Grandview Exchange in your browser, or
  • Open your browser and enter grandviewexchange.groups.io.

Once Grandview Exchange opens in your browser, you may need to log in with your Grandview Exchange account.

  • If you don't have an account yet, click this link to register.
  • If you need help logging in, click this link for assistance.

Once you log in, on the left side of the page, click Subscription.Email settings are right at the top of the page.

Choose the option that works best for you:

  • Individual Emails — You receive every message as it is posted.
  • Full Digest — You receive one email containing all recent messages.
  • Daily Summary — You receive one email per day with a short overview.
  • Special Notices Only — You receive only important announcements from the group owner.

Scroll all the way to the bottom of the page and click Save. Your new email settings will take effect immediately.

You will now begin receiving messages according to your chosen settings and are ready to read, reply, and post using email.
